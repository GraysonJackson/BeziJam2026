## Canon and structural decisions

1. **Rollback remains disabled — Locked.** Choices are meant to matter. The History/Log and a Play help entry will provide rereading and explanation instead.
2. **Madeline and Ulysses previously dated — Locked.** Preserve the approximately one-year relationship, its end last year, and their currently functional coworker relationship. Improve disclosure flow when needed; do not remove the history.
3. **Ulysses throttling Winston in the introduction remains unchanged — Locked.** The introduction is creator-authored canon.
4. **Dhampir kills a squirrel for blood — Locked, polish allowed.** Preserve the event and its dark-comedy purpose. Staging and player reactions may be improved.
5. **Dhampir kills every criminal and ATLAS accepts him — Locked.** Preserve the established black-comedy moral framework. Do not rewrite it into a repudiation arc.
6. **Routes advance by character visit count, not calendar day — Locked.** A first visit always plays that character's Visit 1. Add calendar-aware greetings, deadlines, transitions, and suspect counts so late first/second visits remain coherent.
7. **Suboptimal mixed investigation patterns may remain suboptimal — Locked.** A 2/2/2 split does not receive a new catch-up solution.
8. **The one-visit-with-everyone strategy is the mixed-route exception — Locked.** On Day 6, Ulysses recognizes this exact breadth strategy and formally helps isolate the culprit.
9. **Winston met Ulysses when Ulysses was thirteen — Locked.** Winston was already a superhero. Add a small clarification that their early cooperation was unofficial and later developed into ATLAS.
10. **Nicky's “Good rookie” remains — Locked.** It is an intentional, lightly dominant character beat.
11. **Madeline's profanity remains part of her voice — Locked.** She is loud, angry, punkish, and rough around the edges. Generic repetition may be sharpened without sanitizing her.
12. **Ica's modern-feeling slang remains — Locked.** Preserve lines such as “Cringe” and “Take the L, bozo”; her voice takes priority over strict period slang.
13. **Freddy appears only in the introduction — Locked.** Rewrite his farewell so he explicitly says goodbye forever and does not promise future appearances.
14. **The placeholder main-menu background remains untouched — Deferred.** Art is in production; do not generate or substitute title art in this pass.

## Emotional pacing and route continuity

15. **The audit's Day 2 pacing concern is accepted with moderation.** Do not remove all vulnerability; turn Visit 2 into an early glimpse rather than a complete emotional disclosure.
16. **Deeper personal disclosures move later — Approved.** Visit 2 may seed the issue; Visits 4 or 5 can contain the fuller disclosure after positive relationship development.
17. **Razzle:** Visit 2 may show that being treated as a fire hazard bothers her. Save “accident waiting to happen” depth for later.
18. **Dhampir:** Visit 2 keeps public staring and jokes it away. Save his sincere feelings about being judged for Visit 4.
19. **Madeline:** Visit 2 establishes that harmless failure is enjoyable. Save her fear of people dying because of her mistakes for later.
20. **Nicky:** Visit 2 shows that she notices judgment about her appearance. Save the fuller personal disclosure for later.
21. **Winston:** Visit 2 lightly establishes that others use him as an emergency brake. Save his uncertainty about being genuinely wanted for later.
22. **Later disclosures usually require a relatively positive relationship — Approved.** Lower-affection versions should remain more guarded.
23. **Late-week early visits still occur — Locked.** Rewrite their framing to acknowledge the approaching Day 7 deadline rather than pretending the week has just begun.

## Dialogue choices, flirting, and recurring devices

24. **Remove romantic “Careful, [nickname]” repetition — Approved.** Keep literal uses of “careful” for physical or investigative caution.
25. **Character-specific flirt reactions — Approved.** Razzle escalates or laughs; Dhampir returns strange dark humor; Madeline intellectualizes; Nicky teases confidently; Winston jokes and briefly loses composure; Ica remains nonchalant.
26. **Negative choices become believable viewpoints — Approved.** Replace cartoon cruelty with skepticism, cynicism, caution, impatience, or dry humor where possible.
27. **Four-option menus are allowed — Approved.** Use them when they create meaningfully distinct player attitudes.
28. **Genuinely cruel choices may still lose substantial points — Approved.** Ordinary disagreement and dry humor should be neutral or only mildly negative.
29. **Repeated food theft will be diversified — Approved, implementation delegated.** Keep it only where it feels character-specific; use other intimacy beats elsewhere.
30. **Razzle's “hot and bothered” fire pun remains available — Locked with placement flexibility.** It may be moved or reframed if needed for pacing.

## Ulysses relationship design

31. **Professional trust and romantic interest become separate state — Approved.** Competence alone must not make the protagonist initiate romance.
32. **Every evening report remains mandatory; the personal segment becomes optional — Approved.** Offer a natural “stay a little longer” or “call it a night” choice.
33. **Leaving skips only personal material — Approved.** Reports, route commentary, case state, and required help still occur.
34. **Professional answers build trust; explicit personal/flirtatious answers build romantic interest — Approved.** Some answers may reasonably build both.
35. **Visit 5 hand-holding becomes an explicit player choice — Approved.** Never auto-initiate it from a point threshold.
36. **Visit 6 tie adjustment and near-kiss require a clearly romantic choice — Approved.** Professional gratitude must not trigger romantic physical contact.
37. **Professional Ulysses scenes may still be warm and friendly — Approved.** Removing forced romance must not make him cold.
38. **Replace the post-Dakota killer-name question — Approved.** Ask how he safely communicates useful information instead of immediately probing for a loophole.
39. **Vary the six office evenings — Approved.** All remain in his office, but work, food, lighting, seating, records, interruptions, and activity may change.

## Relationship thresholds and endings

40. **Target optimal minimum visits for romance — Approved starting calibration.** Easy routes (Razzle, Winston): about 3 excellent visits. Medium routes (Nicky, Ica): about 4. Harder routes (Ulysses, Madeline, Dhampir): about 5. These are difficulty targets, not perfection requirements.
41. **Friendship generally requires two visits — Locked.** One visit alone should not normally unlock a character friendship ending.
42. **One visit with everyone does not automatically grant friendship — Implementation delegated.** A romantic ask after only one visit should generally receive a kind, character-specific rejection rather than a friendship ending.
43. **Mixed-route romance remains possible — Locked.** Concentrating roughly three or four visits on one character with strong choices can succeed according to that character's difficulty.
44. **Ulysses remains harder to romance — Locked.** Recalibrate his threshold around the new trust/romantic-interest split.
