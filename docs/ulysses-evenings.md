# Ulysses Evening Route

Ulysses is not a daytime selection. The player reports to him privately in his
office at the end of all six investigation days. Every evening combines three
layers:

1. A unique report for the selected investigator's current visit. Six
   investigators with six visits each produce thirty-six bespoke report scenes.
2. A response to the player's distribution of time: meeting the full team,
   developing a focus, changing focus, or repeatedly avoiding work with Ica.
3. One part of Ulysses's six-evening personal and romantic arc.

The report scenes reward accurate, contextual, and honest reporting. Taking
credit from a partner, disguising failure, overclaiming uncertain evidence, or
pressuring Ulysses to reveal the future damages the relationship. Competence is
necessary for romance, but the highest threshold also requires subtle flirting,
respect for his boundaries, and attention to the person beneath his role.

## Personal progression

- **Evening 1:** Office routine, Winston's standing pizza order, jazz, and the
  player's first chance to stay and help.
- **Evening 2:** Team contingency files and the story of Ulysses meeting
  Winston during a forest-fire mission in Montana. ATLAS became official when
  Ulysses was fifteen.
- **Evening 3:** The rules of his continuous future sight. Large events remain
  fixed while small details update; no future event can be communicated safely.
- **Evening 4:** The seizure and four-month coma caused by an attempted future
  disclosure when he was nineteen, followed by a quieter discussion of why he
  sometimes hates his power.
- **Evening 5:** The player convinces him to stop working. Music, reading, chess,
  and optional hand-holding let him spend time with somebody without treating
  the interaction as another responsibility.
- **Evening 6:** Final case organization and the strongest pre-confession
  romantic beat. There is no kiss or request for a date before Day 7.

## One-each strategy

If the player spends exactly one day with Razzle, Dhampir, Madeline, Nicky,
Winston, and Ica, the five investigative first visits leave four suspects. On
Evening 6, Ulysses performs a cross-report analysis with the player. A retryable
dialogue deduction and optional intuition hint identify the only complete
profile consistent with every report. The resulting formal clue removes the
other three suspects and leaves only the seeded killer.

This is a deliberate reward for collecting data from every category. Ulysses
does not supply equivalent rescue evidence for inefficient distributions; the
player remains responsible for the consequences of how they spent the week.
